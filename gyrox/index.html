GYROX

Architected solid oxide electrochemical systems

GYROX replaces the planar solid oxide stack with a single printed body of functional metal oxide. The electrolyte is a zirconia gyroid, a triply periodic minimal surface that separates two interpenetrating gas channels. Coated with fuel and oxygen electrodes, the same body works as a fuel cell or as an electrolyser.

Fabrication

In order of production.

  1. Design

    A digital gyroid model, sized for wall thickness and channel width, sliced into thin layers.

  2. Print

    Lithography-based ceramic manufacturing (LCM) of the 8YSZ body as one piece, with thin gastight walls.

  3. Coat

    Fuel and oxygen electrode inks applied to the two channel networks.

  4. Co-sinter and test

    Firing with the sealed shell frame, then operation in fuel cell and electrolysis mode.

Outlook

Mass-first design

Aerospace and space

Where every gram counts and nothing can be repaired, specific power and monolithic robustness matter more than peak efficiency. Architecture becomes the main design variable.
