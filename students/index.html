MSc thesisThin films · Ferroelectrics
Open

Nanosecond laser annealing of hafnia–zirconia thin films for ferroelectric memory

Can a short laser pulse give an oxide film the crystal structure it needs to store a bit? You will map laser energy against crystallization, leakage and switchable polarisation in HZO capacitors.

Film, 41 s · figure and full description on the project page

MSc thesisThin films · Ferroelectrics
Open

Retention of ferroelectric memory states in hafnia–zirconia thin films

Write a bit, switch off the voltage, read it back later. You will measure how long the two polarisation states of HZO capacitors stay distinguishable, and whether one direction fades faster than the other.

Film, 41 s · figure and full description on the project page

MSc thesisArchitected oxides · Piezoelectrics
Open

Testing 3D-printed piezoelectric ceramics as three-axis force sensors for robotic grippers

Can a printed KNN gyroid fingertip tell a squeeze from a sideways pull? You will build a three-channel sensor, calibrate it against a reference, and test it on a gripping and sliding task.

Film, 41 s · figure and full description on the project page

MSc thesisArchitected oxides · Solid oxide cells
Open

Testing 3D-printed solid oxide fuel cells for compact power generation

How tightly can the channels of a gyroid fuel cell be packed before fuel supply eats the gain in electrode area? You will compare a coarse and a fine design on hydrogen and air.

Film, 41 s · figure and full description on the project page

MSc thesisArchitected oxides · Piezoelectrics · Modelling
Open

Tuning 3D-printed piezoelectric gyroids as ultrasonic resonators

Same space, same ceramic, different frequency? In COMSOL you will map how cell pitch and wall thickness of a printed KNN gyroid set its resonances, and which ones the electrodes can drive.
